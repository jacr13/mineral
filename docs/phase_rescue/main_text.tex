An offline paired diagnostic on held-out expert windows shows that
augmenting a deliberately phase-mismatched candidate with seven uniformly
sampled alternatives allows the minimum-OT-cost candidate to be
phase-compatible in 46--72\% of query--pool pairs across four environments
(Appendix~\ref{sec:phase_rescue}). This tests minimum-cost candidate
selection; it does not establish recovery during policy training or
validate the soft-weighted gradient.
